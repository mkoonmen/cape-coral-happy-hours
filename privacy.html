Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Cape Coral Happy Hours does not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

When it opens, the app downloads a public list of restaurants from GitHub. GitHub may log the request (for example your IP address) as any website does; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Tapping Directions opens your maps app, Call opens your phone app, and Suggest an update opens a public form on GitHub. Anything you submit there is public and handled under GitHub's terms.

Happy hour details come from public sources and can change without notice. Confirm with the restaurant.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/mkoonmen/cape-coral-happy-hours.
